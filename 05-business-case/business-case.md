# Business Case: Fable Growth

> Module 5 · Build the Business Case and the Kill Line

**Rock:** The steady plan and self-check (Rock 1, from backlog #6). The ask: fund a one-quarter concierge test, and the build only if it passes.

## 1. The strategic bet

> Recovered premium users who still use sleep content keep a nightly wind-down built from what helped them, so more of them are still paying at day 180. Each one kept is about $150 of margin that Fable now loses when the hard stretch ends.

## 2. The assumptions, ranked

1. People who have recovered want an ongoing plan and will keep paying for it (the concierge test's opt-in and paid return).
2. A rules-based steady plan keeps most of the concierge test's lift once it's automated. The hand-built plans set the upper limit.
3. About 20,000 monthly-plan deep users reach day 90 each month, so a 20-point lift means about 4,000 more paying at day 180 per cohort (est.; checkable in Fable's data now).

## 3. The expected return

**Per customer:** Each extra monthly-plan user still paying at day 180 is worth about $150 of margin over the next 18 months ($12 a month, after app-store fees; est.).

**Volume target:** 4,000 more monthly-plan users still paying at day 180 in each monthly cohort by month 12 (KR1 from 35% to 55%), about $7.5M of margin a year against about $2.2M for the team of 14.

## 4. The kill criterion

> If fewer than 35% of concierge participants use the wind-down three or more nights a week by week 6, or fewer than 10% of churned participants resume paying, or the regulatory opinion doesn't clear self-checks, by the end of month 3, we stop the steady plan build, keep only the bedtime reminder and load-time fixes, and return the unspent initiative budget for Next and Later to the board.

## 5. The model behind it

Every input is an estimate, since the scenario gives no price or cohort sizes; Module 5 replaces them with Fable's own data.

| Input | Value |
|---|---|
| Price | $12 a month; 70% left after app-store fees and running costs |
| Value of each extra user kept to day 180 | About $150: 2 more months to day 180, plus about 17 months after it at 6% monthly churn |
| Monthly-plan deep users reaching day 90 | 20,000 a month |
| Team cost | 14 × $160k fully loaded = $2.24M a year |

| KR1 lift | Extra users per monthly cohort | Annual margin | Return : cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 points (plan, 35% → 55%) | 4,000 | $7.5M | 3.4x |
| 16 points (20% below plan) | 3,200 | $6.0M | 2.7x |
| 14 points (30% below plan) | 2,800 | $5.3M | 2.4x |
| Break-even: 6 points (KR1 at 41%) | 1,200 | $2.2M | 1.0x |

The Now quarter puts about $560k at risk, and the kill criterion stops spending there if the concierge test fails. Annual-plan users and the free-to-premium path in Later are not counted. How it was built: [NOTES.md](NOTES.md).
