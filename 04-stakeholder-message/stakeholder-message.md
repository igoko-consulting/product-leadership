# Stakeholder Message: Fable Growth

> Module 4 · Frame the Story That Gets Executive Buy-in

## 1. The hard no

> **Hard no:** We are not building a community and social layer or progress sharing.
>
> **Stakeholder:** The Growth team lead

## 2. The message

> Growth co-owns the bedtime reminder we ship this quarter, and any message we send someone before bedtime is a joint call between you and PM 2. We are not building a community and social layer or progress sharing, and that decision is made; the daily streak is off the plan too. The only social work on the roadmap is a one-to-one buddy test, led by PM 2 and co-designed with you, run only if day-180 retention stays flat for two monthly cohorts after the steady plan launches; I make that call. I know you pushed for social features and streaks to keep people longer and grow through referrals, and this takes them away while your targets stay the same. The reason is the people we serve: even fully staffed, sharing mental-health progress puts people at risk when they're most vulnerable. On Thursday I'll meet you and PM 2 to agree the reminder plan and how we'll measure a referral invite Growth can run this quarter that shares nothing about anyone's use, and you'll get the day-180 numbers at each monthly cohort review.

The hard no is Module 2's hard no 2 and Module 1's hard no, the one that matters most to Growth: a social layer was their answer from the start. Each part follows the four steps for saying no. The message was revised after both models read it as the Growth lead: the two phrases that left the no open are gone, the one condition for the buddy test is named, Growth's unchanged targets get a referral invite of their own, and the wording uses the repo's terms. A clean re-check in both models then closed the last openings: a defined plateau, a reason about risk rather than headcount, and the streak named.
