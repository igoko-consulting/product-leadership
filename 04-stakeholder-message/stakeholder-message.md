# Stakeholder Message: Fable Growth

> Module 4 · Frame the Story That Gets Executive Buy-in

## 1. The hard no

> **Hard no:** We are not building a community and social layer or progress sharing.
>
> **Stakeholder:** The Growth team lead

## 2. The message

> Growth co-owns the bedtime reminder we ship this quarter, and any message we send someone before bedtime is a joint call between you and PM 2. We are not building a community and social layer or progress sharing, and that decision is made. The only social work on the roadmap is a one-to-one buddy test, led by PM 2 and co-designed with you, which runs only if day-180 retention plateaus once the steady plan is live. I know you pushed for social features and streaks to keep people longer and grow through referrals, and this takes them away while your targets stay the same. The reason is the people we serve: sharing mental-health progress needs safeguarding a team of 14 can't staff. On Thursday I'll meet you and PM 2 to agree the reminder plan and a referral invite Growth can run this quarter that shares nothing about anyone's use, and you'll see the retention numbers when I do.

The hard no is Module 2's hard no 2 and Module 1's hard no, the one that matters most to Growth: a social layer was their answer from the start. Each part follows the four steps for saying no. The message was revised after both models read it as the Growth lead: the two phrases that left the no open are gone, the one condition for the buddy test is named, Growth's unchanged targets get a referral invite of their own, and the wording uses the repo's terms.
