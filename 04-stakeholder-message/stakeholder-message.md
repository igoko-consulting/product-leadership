# Stakeholder Message: Fable Growth

> Module 4 · Frame the Story That Gets Executive Buy-in

## 1. The hard no

> **Hard no:** We are not building a social layer or progress sharing.
>
> **Stakeholder:** The Growth team lead

## 2. The message

> Growth co-owns the bedtime reminder we ship this quarter, and any message we send someone before bedtime is a joint call between you and PM 2. We are not building a social layer or progress sharing, and that decision is made. The only social work on the roadmap is a one-to-one buddy test, led by PM 2 and co-designed with Growth, which runs only if day-180 retention stalls once the steady plan is live. I know you've pushed for social, streaks and referrals because you see them as the way to keep people longer and grow without paid acquisition, and taking them off this plan changes the approach you've argued for while your targets stay the same. The reason is the people we serve: sharing mental-health progress needs safeguarding a team of 14 can't staff. On Thursday I'll meet you and PM 2 to agree the bedtime reminder plan and a privacy-safe referral route Growth can run this quarter, and you'll see the day-180 retention numbers at the same time I do.

The hard no is Module 2's hard no 2 and Module 1's hard no, the one that matters most to Growth: a social layer was their answer from the start. Each part follows the four steps for saying no. The message was revised after both models read it as the Growth lead: the two phrases that left the no open are gone, the one condition for the buddy test is named, and Growth's unchanged targets get a referral route of their own.
