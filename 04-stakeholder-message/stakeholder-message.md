> **Stakeholder:** The Growth team lead

## 2. The message

> Growth co-owns the bedtime reminder we ship this quarter, the change most likely to keep people with us, and any message we send someone before bedtime is a joint call between you and PM 2. We are not building a social layer, sharing progress with friends included, in this initiative, and that decision is made. I know a social layer has been Growth's answer to retention from the start, and that this, with the daily streak, takes two of your asks off the plan and the referral loop that came with them. The reason is the people we serve: sharing mental-health progress needs safeguarding a team of 14 can't staff while we prove the steady plan. This week I'll book an hour with you and PM 2 to agree the bedtime reminder plan and the messaging rules you co-own, and you'll see the day-180 retention numbers at the same time I do.
