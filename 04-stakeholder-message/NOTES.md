## Labs

### Hard no to a stakeholder

- **The stakeholder (from the Lab Guide):** the Growth team lead. Growth has a stake in PM 3's AI check-in layer and wants both the daily streak and a social layer.
- **Chosen, the social layer:** it was Growth's answer from the start of the scenario and is the Module 1 hard no, so this message settles the biggest open fight. Their resistance is about interest (the referral growth it would have brought) and identity (the bet they're known for). The daily streak was the easier no, with less at stake. Missed: both are Growth's asks, so whichever no is sent, the other still has to be said.
- **The message:** four parts in the order the slides give: what stays open (Growth co-owns the bedtime reminder, and pre-bedtime messages are a joint call), the plain no, the cost to them, and a next step. Plain words throughout, since Module 2 flagged the course jargon as a problem for anyone outside the team. The gated buddy experiment was left out so the no couldn't be reopened. Missed: Growth can see the roadmap, so leaving the buddy test out could look like hiding it, which the AI check confirmed.
- **The optional AI check:** run in both models ([replies](ai-replies/1-ai-check.md)). Both found "in this initiative" leaves the door open; Claude also caught "while we prove the steady plan"; both said the message names Growth's lost features but not their unchanged targets. Claude's chat had earlier context, so its read isn't clean, but its point about the buddy test holds.
- **Revised after the check:** the no has no time limit ("in this initiative" and "while we prove the steady plan" are gone); the buddy test is named with its one condition, led by PM 2 and co-designed with Growth; the message acknowledges Growth's goal and unchanged targets, not just their features; and the meeting has a day and two outcomes, the bedtime reminder plan and a privacy-safe referral route Growth runs this quarter. The opening still starts with what stays open, as the slides teach, although Claude read it as softening. Missed: at about 170 words it is long for a hard no, but each sentence answers one of the three read-back checks.
- **Tightened before a re-check:** the revision named referrals as taken away and then offered a referral route, so referrals now stay with Growth; the route became a referral invite that shares nothing about anyone's use; the wording uses the repo's terms ("community and social layer", "plateaus"); and the message dropped to about 160 words. Missed: the first revision was approved with the contradiction in it, which a second read as the stakeholder would have caught, hence the clean re-check.
- **Clean re-check, then stop:** both models read the tightened message in new memory-off chats ([replies](ai-replies/2-recheck.md)). Neither found a maybe in the main no; GPT-6 scored it 9 out of 10. The last fixes: "plateau" defined as two flat monthly cohorts and called by me, a reason about risk to people rather than headcount, the daily streak named as off the plan, a measure for the referral invite, and the day-180 numbers at each monthly cohort review. Missed: Growth will raise its unchanged targets on Thursday whatever the message says, and the charter sends that to the CPO, so the answer is prepared rather than written into the no.
- **The three read-back checks, after the revision:** a no that can be repeated ("We are not building a social layer or progress sharing, and that decision is made"); the need and the cost acknowledged (their approach and their unchanged targets); and a specific path (Thursday, the reminder plan, a measured referral invite, the day-180 numbers at each monthly cohort review).
