- **The stakeholder (from the Lab Guide):** the Growth team lead. Growth has a stake in PM 3's AI check-in layer and wants both the daily streak and a social layer.
- **Chosen, the social layer:** it was Growth's answer from the start of the scenario and is the Module 1 hard no, so this message settles the biggest open fight. Their resistance is about interest (the referral growth it would have brought) and identity (the bet they're known for). The daily streak was the easier no, with less at stake. Missed: both are Growth's asks, so whichever no is sent, the other still has to be said.

Plain words throughout, since Module 2 flagged the course jargon as a problem for anyone outside the team. The gated buddy experiment was left out so the no couldn't be reopened. Missed: Growth can see the roadmap, so leaving the buddy test out could look like hiding it, which the AI check confirmed.
