# Team Charter

> Module 3 · Set the Habits That Make a Team Faster

## 1. What We Own

> **We own:** The post-acute experience from day 60 to day 180: the steady plan (hand-built concierge plans now, rules-based v1 next), the nightly wind-down and its load time, the bedtime reminder jointly with Growth, and the self-checks users start themselves, with PM 2 accountable for KR1–KR3. PM 1 owns days 0–60, including the first time a user is shown the wind-down, and PM 3 owns the AI check-in layer as a shared platform, but what that layer shows a user after day 60 is PM 2's call.
>
> **Out of scope:** New-user acquisition, paid and app-store marketing, and all lifecycle messaging other than the bedtime reminder (win-back emails, re-engagement campaigns) belong to Growth. Diagnosis, urgent support and clinical care belong to Clinical advisory and the signposting partner; consent and data policy belongs to Legal/Privacy; and employer or insurer offers belong to Commercial and stay out of scope until post-acute retention and willingness to pay are proven.
>
> **Joint calls:** Before anyone builds or ships, PM 1 and PM 2 jointly agree any change to days 0–60 that affects what the steady plan can use, and PM 2 and Growth jointly agree any message to a deep user in the three hours before bedtime. Any new self-check prompt, trigger or wording needs PM 3, PM 2 and Clinical advisory to sign off together; Fable never starts a check-in, and AI personalisation waits until steady plan v1 shows a retention lift. The share of Growth, Legal/Privacy and Data/Insights time the initiative gets is agreed with their leads each quarter.

## 2. How We Decide

> **Who decides scope:** Within the roadmap, the PM who owns the metric a feature moves decides its scope without needing approval: PM 1 for activation, PM 2 for KR1–KR3 and PM 3 for check-in accuracy and response time. Clinical advisory can veto anything that could read as diagnosis or monitoring, and the engineering lead decides how and when work is built, not whether. Only the Fable product lead can move work between Now, Next and Later, add a fourth Now bet, or override a kill criterion such as Bet 1's stop rule.
>
> **How cross-team conflicts escalate:** Either party raises the conflict within two working days of spotting it, in a one-page decision note (the options, the KR each affects, and their recommendation), and the people involved have three working days to settle it. If they can't, the Fable product lead decides within two working days of reading the note, or the CPO does when Growth is a party, using one test: which option better protects day-180 retention for deep users without crossing the clinical line. The decision is logged and everyone commits to it; a conflict about clinical safety goes to the product lead and the clinical lead the same day, and the safer option holds until they decide.
