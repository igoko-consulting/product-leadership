### Strategic sequencing

The roadmap deliberately makes three choices:

1. Now: Establish the nightly habit. Make Fable valuable during ordinary life, not just difficult periods. Remove the friction that prevents that habit forming.
2. Next: Deepen the relationship. Introduce user-controlled personalisation, self-reflection and a broader sense of progress once the core wind-down behaviour is established.
3. Later: Explore additional growth engines. Consider social accountability, new audiences and partnerships only after the post-acute proposition demonstrates traction.

One important leadership distinction: Your three quarterly rocks are delivery commitments; the Now column translates them into strategic bets. The fake-door test and regulatory opinion are validation gates, not separate strategic outcomes.

I would also resist treating AI-generated check-ins as the centrepiece of the initiative. The strategic bet is that Fable becomes valuable for maintaining progress. AI is simply one possible means of delivering that value.

**What I changed and why:**

- **Rock 1 contradicts the strategy.** "AI-generated daily check-in prompts" are started by Fable, which is the monitoring you've ruled out. So I've framed the Now bet as validating demand, and the self-checks as something the user starts, in Next. Consider renaming the rock to match, or your board will spot the gap.

- **Capacity.** With 14 people, Now uses roughly half the team on engineering fixes and half on validating demand. Nothing in Next starts until Bet 1 passes its signal. That gate is what makes this a roadmap and not a backlog.
- **Placeholder targets.** The thresholds in the success signals (15%, 5%, 98%, 30% and so on) are my suggested starting points, not numbers from your data. Set them against your own baselines.
