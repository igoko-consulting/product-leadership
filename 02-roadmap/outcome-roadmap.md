# Outcome Roadmap & Trade-off Memo

> Module 2 · Turn Your Strategy into an Outcome Roadmap

## 1. Trade-off memo

> **Rock 1:** The steady plan and the self-check the user starts, from backlog #6 (Add AI-generated daily check-in prompts personalized to user history) (supports KR3 and KR1). It draws on what helped each user before, so they feel steady through their own plan rather than chased by Fable; a concierge test comes first, with the content team hand-building plans for a small consenting group of churned and current users, and a regulatory opinion before launch.
>
> **Rock 2:** Fix the notification system (wrong times, timezone bug) (supports KR2). The nightly wind-down reminder has to land at bedtime in the user's own timezone or it trains people to ignore it, and wrong-time pings are what drive the opt-outs the guardrail watches.
>
> **Rock 3:** Reduce app load time from 4.2s to under 2s on 4G (supports KR2). At bedtime a 4.2-second load is a reason to give up; under 2 seconds keeps the wind-down effortless for every user.
>
> **Hard no 1:** Launch a premium tier with therapist-matching. It moves Fable towards the clinical positioning it can't take, serves the acute phase rather than the post-acute user, and is the biggest build on the list.
>
> **Hard no 2:** Build a social layer: share your progress with friends. Sharing mental-health progress with friends brings privacy and safeguarding risk that a team of 14 can't carry alongside the steady plan; a one-to-one buddy experiment runs only if KR1 plateaus once the steady plan is live.
>
> **Hard no 3:** Add a daily streak feature to drive habit formation. It rewards daily opens, the nudging both models warned about in Module 1, and works against KR3's "without feeling dependent"; the wind-down counts nights a week, not unbroken days.

The table is Claude's final roadmap with the Growth team added to Bet 2, refined with both models' reviews: denominators and a paid rejoin for Bet 1, outcomes ahead of the delivery guardrail for Bet 2, a rules-based steady plan first, a KR3 mid-point, one lead team per item, a signal for every Later bet, and no clinical wording. Bet 1 then swapped its fake door for a concierge test, so people who left after a hard stretch get something real, with consent asked first. Now proves demand, Next proves behaviour and retention, Later proves lasting value and advantage. Growth, Legal/Privacy, Data/Insights and Commercial are shared services lent to the initiative, not new hires.
